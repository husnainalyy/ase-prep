# Day 1 — Computer Networks

**Topic:** Networking Fundamentals

---

## 1. What is a computer network?

### Answer

A computer network is a group of devices connected so they can share data and resources.

- The devices can be computers, phones, printers, and servers.
- They share files, messages, printers, and the internet connection.
- The link can be a cable or Wi-Fi.
- They follow shared rules, called protocols, so each device understands the other.
- A home router with a laptop and a phone on Wi-Fi is a small network.

### Interview

A computer network is two or more devices connected so they can share data and resources. The connection can be a cable or Wi-Fi. At home, the laptop, the phone, and the printer all talk through the router. That group of connected devices is a computer network.

#### Example

In an office, every computer uses one printer and one shared folder. The computers are connected through the office router, so each desk does not need its own printer.

## 2. Explain LAN, MAN, and WAN.

### Answer

These names tell you how big the network is.

- **LAN (Local Area Network):** one home, one school, or one office. It is small, fast, and private.
- **MAN (Metropolitan Area Network):** one city or a large campus. It joins several LANs in that city.
- **WAN (Wide Area Network):** a country or the whole world. It joins networks that are far apart. The Internet is the largest WAN.
- Memory line: local is a building, metropolitan is a city, wide is the world.

### Interview

LAN, MAN, and WAN describe the size of a network. A LAN covers a small place such as a home or one office. A MAN covers a city and connects several LANs there. A WAN covers a large area, such as a country or the world. The Internet is a WAN.

#### Example

The Wi-Fi in one office is a LAN. A bank that links every branch inside one city uses a MAN. The same bank linking offices in different countries uses a WAN.

## 3. What is the difference between the Internet and an intranet?

### Answer

The Internet is public and open to the world. An intranet is a private network for one organization.

- The Internet connects networks across the world. Anyone with access can use it.
- An intranet uses similar web pages and tools, but only people inside the company or school can open it.
- The intranet holds private work, such as salaries, marks, or internal news.
- A company can use the Internet and still keep the intranet closed to outsiders.
- One step further, if they ask: an extranet is a private network that also lets selected partners in, such as a supplier.

### Interview

The Internet is the public global network. Anyone can connect and open public sites. An intranet is a private network inside one organization. Staff use it for internal pages and files, and people outside cannot open it. The tools look similar. The intranet stays closed to the public.

#### Example

Google and public email are on the Internet. A company page where staff check salary slips and leave requests is an intranet. A person outside the company cannot log in to that page.
